Candy Cascade

Swap candies, match 3 or more, and chain big combos!

How to play: drag a candy (or tap two neighbours) to swap. Match 3+ in a row.
4 in a row → striped candy (clears a line). L / T shape → wrapped candy (3×3 blast). 5 in a row → color bomb (swap it with any candy to wipe that color). Combine specials for huge combos!
