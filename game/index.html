Specter Cascade

Swap restless spirits, match 3 or more, and unleash haunting combos in Specter, Georgia.

How to play: drag a spirit (or tap two neighbours) to swap. Match 3+ in a row.
4 in a row → striped spirit (clears a line). L / T shape → wrapped spirit (3×3 blast). 5 in a row → spirit orb (swap it with anything to banish that whole kind). Combine specials for huge combos!
